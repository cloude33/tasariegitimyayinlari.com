# Product Context

## Register
brand

## Users
Students preparing for Turkish national exams (DGS, YÖS, ALES, KPSS). They seek premium, high-trust, professional, and modern educational resources.

## Brand
Tasarı Eğitim Yayınları – Lider ve köklü eğitim kurumu (Since 1984).

## Tone
Premium, high-trust, elite, modern, clean, authoritative.

## Anti-references
Bland templates, boring identical card grids, corporate blue clichés, messy layouts.

## Strategic Principles
Visual excellence, high typographic contrast, fluid responsive structures.
